% Abstract
\begin{center}
\begin{abstract}

We search for $B$ mesons decaying to final states containing
two baryons: $\Bz \rightarrow \Lambda^0 \Lambda^0$ and
$\Bu \rightarrow \Lambda_c^+ \Lambda^0$ (charge conjugation implied).
These decays violate baryon number by $\pm 2$
and would be a signature of physics beyond the standard model.
The searches use the full \babar\ dataset and follow the strategy of the
completed $\Bu \rightarrow \bar{p} \Lambda^0$ analysis (BAD \#2723):
a blind analysis in the \mes/\DeltaE\ plane with upper limits computed
using a Rolke-style procedure.
\textbf{[DRAFT: analysis in progress; the collision-data signal regions
remain blinded.]}
\end{abstract}
\end{center}
